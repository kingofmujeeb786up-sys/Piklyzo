About Piklyzo Image Optimizer

Piklyzo is a free browser-based image compressor and resizer designed for photos used in online applications, forms, documents and everyday image optimization.

Image processing is performed locally in your browser. The selected image is not intentionally uploaded to a Piklyzo server by this page.
