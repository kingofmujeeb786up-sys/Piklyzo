About Piklyzo Image Optimizer

Piklyzo is the world's most comprehensive online image utility designed for job aspirants and students. It offers instant KB compression, custom form naming, date stamping, professional borders, and direct sharing without app installations.

How to Optimize Photos for Forms?

1. Upload your JPG or PNG photo securely.
2. Choose target KB size, dimensions, and check stamp or border options.
3. Download instantly as JPG/PDF or share directly with a single click.
