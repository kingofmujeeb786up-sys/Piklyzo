About Piklyzo Image Optimizer

Piklyzo is an ultra-fast, secure online image compression utility designed to help users instantly resize and shrink photo dimensions to specific KB sizes without losing quality.

How to Reduce Photo Size in KB?

1. Upload your PNG, JPG, or WEBP photo using the file picker above.
2. Set your custom file target in KB (e.g., 20KB, 50KB, 100KB).
3. Click on the Download button to save your resized photo instantly.

Fast & Secure Browser-Side Compression

All photo processing happens natively inside your browser canvas engine. No images are ever uploaded to an external server, keeping your data completely private and safe.
