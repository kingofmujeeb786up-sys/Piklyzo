What is Piklyzo?

Piklyzo is a free online image utility for people who need to compress, resize and prepare photos for applications, forms, websites and everyday sharing. The editor runs in your browser, helping keep the processing lightweight and private.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Piklyzo upload my photos?

The image processing in this page is performed locally in your browser. Piklyzo does not need an image-upload server for these editing operations.

Can I compress an image to an exact KB size?

The compressor aims for your requested KB size by adjusting image quality and dimensions. Because image formats work differently, the final file size can be slightly above or below the target.

Can I process multiple images?

Yes. Select multiple images and use Process All. Your browser processes them sequentially to reduce memory pressure.

Does background removal work on every photo?

The built-in background remover is designed for simple, mostly uniform backgrounds. It is not an AI subject-segmentation system, so complex backgrounds may require a dedicated AI tool.
